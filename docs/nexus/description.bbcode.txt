[size=5][b]Pyramids Custom Maps: the Great Cat and Sobek[/b][/size]
[i]Why build a pyramid when you could build a giant stone statue?[/i]

[b]Pyramids Custom Maps[/b] adds custom maps to [i]How They Built the Pyramids[/i]. Each one swaps the pyramid for a giant statue that you build brick by brick. Building it is the whole objective, from the first brick on the ground to the last brick at the very top, and then the ending.

[size=4][b]The maps[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]The Great Cat[/b]: a stone cat, crouched and ready to pounce. 24,052 blocks (about as many as the pyramid) and 37.5 m tall. The last brick goes on the tip of her left ear.
[*][b]Sobek[/b] [i](new in 0.4.0)[/i]: a giant crocodile lying in an oval pool, jaws open to the sky. 19,538 blocks, 21 m tall and about 130 m long. Climb into his open mouth to build the raised upper jaw; the last brick is the tip of his snout. [i]Honest note: Sobek is new and hasn't had a full playthrough yet. If a spot can't be built or something looks off, please report it![/i]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Smoother shapes[/b]: statue blocks are half the size of a pyramid block, so the curves come out smooth.
[*][b]Build in any order[/b]: every block is open from the start. A block must touch one you've already laid (or sit on the ground layer), so there are no floating bricks. Overhangs like the cat's chin or Sobek's upper jaw grow out from their neighbours.
[*][b]Stack as you go[/b]: you can place a block one layer above your feet, so you build up beside your work and climb onto it.
[*][b]Build markers[/b]: small markers show the spots next to your bricks where you can build next. F7 turns them on and off.
[*][b]Shabti builders[/b]: put a Work Post up on the statue next to a brick crate and they build that level. A Work Post on the ground beside its base builds the bottom layer.
[*][b]Sobek's pool[/b]: a shallow oval pool (0.6 m deep) dug around the build site, using the river's own water. Wade through it; machines can stand in the shallows.
[*][b]The full ending[/b] on both maps. Climb up together and place the last brick.
[*][b]Map chooser in the main menu[/b]: the empty plot gets [b]Great Cat[/b] and [b]Sobek[/b] buttons next to Pyramid and Grand Pyramid. Your existing worlds are never changed.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]How They Built the Pyramids, [b]game version 0.43.0[/b]. Other versions may break the mod; it warns in the log.
[*][url=https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx/releases]BepInEx 5.4.23.6[/url] for Windows x64 ([i]BepInEx_win_x64_5.4.23.6.zip[/i]).
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]In Steam, right-click [i]How They Built the Pyramids[/i] and choose [i]Manage > Browse local files[/i] to open the game folder.
[*]Extract the BepInEx zip into that folder ([i]winhttp.dll[/i] should end up next to [i]Pyramid.exe[/i]).
[*]Extract this mod's zip into the same folder. It adds [i]BepInEx\plugins\PyramidsMaps\PyramidsMaps.dll[/i].
[*]Start the game. On the main menu, scroll to the empty plot, pick [b]Great Cat[/b] or [b]Sobek[/b], and start a new world.
[/list]
[b]Updating from 0.3.0:[/b] extract over the old version. Your cat worlds keep working.
To uninstall, delete the [i]BepInEx\plugins\PyramidsMaps[/i] folder. Statue worlds won't load correctly without the mod.

[size=4][b]Multiplayer[/b][/size]
Everyone who plays together needs the mod, at the same version. The host's world tells joining players which statue it is, and their game builds it too. Players still on 0.3.0 can join cat worlds, but need 0.4.0 for Sobek. [i]Honest note: multiplayer is built in but hasn't been tested with two players yet. Please report how it goes![/i]

[size=4][b]Good to know[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Stone ramps, scaffold platforms and the sledge lift aren't sold in statue worlds, since they only fit pyramid steps.
[*]Keep brick crates off spots that still need a block, or that spot stays blocked.
[*]Statue worlds are kept off the game's online world rankings, so a modded statue never competes with real pyramids.
[*]Steam achievements still unlock in statue worlds.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Bug reports[/b][/size]
Press [b]F8[/b] in game to write a placement and Shabti report to [i]BepInEx\LogOutput.log[/i]. Please attach that file and [i]%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\PattezGames\How They Built the Pyramids\Player.log[/i].

[size=4][b]Source[/b][/size]
The mod is open source on GitHub: [url=https://github.com/kaesoclarus/pyramids-custom-maps]kaesoclarus/pyramids-custom-maps[/url]. More maps can be added there.
